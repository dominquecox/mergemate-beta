Questions, feedback, or a signup issue? Email support@mergemate.dev. Please do not send passwords, access tokens, private keys or private repository content.

Quick answers

When does it launch?

Soon. Waitlist members hear first.

Does MergeMate change my code?

No. It has read-only access and never merges, approves or comments.

Which repositories can it see?

Only the ones you, or your organization admin, choose on GitHub.

Will there be a free plan?

Yes. There will be a free plan and a Pro plan for heavy reviewers.

How do I leave the waitlist?

Use the link in your confirmation email, or write to support@mergemate.dev.

I did not receive a confirmation

Check spam and make sure you entered the correct address. If the email still does not arrive, contact support. Rejoining with the same address does not send another confirmation.

Leave the waitlist

Use the removal link in your confirmation email. If you cannot find it, email support@mergemate.dev from the address you signed up with and ask to be removed.

Delete an app account

When the app is available, use its account deletion option. You can also read how to request deletion or ask by email.
