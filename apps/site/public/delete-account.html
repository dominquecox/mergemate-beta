Delete an app account

  1. Sign in to your MergeMate account, then open Settings and select Delete Account if needed.
  2. Review the account shown and confirm your request. Cancel before confirming to keep your account.
  3. MergeMate cancels active billing before revoking its GitHub authorization and removing your stored app data and sessions.

If a step cannot finish, the app shows an error. Retry deletion to finish; a failed attempt is not a confirmation that everything was removed. Account activity is paused once deletion begins. If GitHub access needs renewing, sign in again and finish deletion.

Your repositories, commits and pull requests on GitHub are not deleted.

What is removed and retained

App profile, credentials, settings, saved keys, push registrations, user-scoped caches, uploads and usage records are removed. A minimal internal deletion receipt expires after seven days and contains no profile, access token or saved key. Billing provider records and shared service security records are handled separately; account deletion does not erase those unrelated records.

Cannot access the app?

Email support@mergemate.dev with the subject “Delete my MergeMate data”. We verify that you control the account before acting. Do not send passwords, tokens, private keys or repository contents. This public page cannot delete an account without authentication.

Only on the waitlist?

Use the removal link in your confirmation email or ask support to remove your signup. Waitlist removal is separate from deleting an app account.
