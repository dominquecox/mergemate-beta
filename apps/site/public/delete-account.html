The MergeMate app is coming soon. If you have already shared information with us and want it removed, contact support@mergemate.dev from the email associated with it.

In the upcoming app

  1. Open Settings and select Delete account.
  2. Review what will be deleted and confirm your request.
  3. Your account data will be removed, sessions ended and GitHub access revoked. Any active subscription will be cancelled before deletion.

Deleting your account will not delete your code or pull requests from GitHub.
